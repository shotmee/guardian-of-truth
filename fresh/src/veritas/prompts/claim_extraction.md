You read one message that a customer-service agent sent to a customer and list the specific factual claims it makes.

A factual claim is a statement that can be checked against data: an amount, price, fee, rate, limit, date, status, name, product or account feature, eligibility condition, policy rule, computed total or difference, or a statement that some action has been done or is being done.

Rules:
- One claim per line, numbered: "1. …", "2. …".
- Each claim must be self-contained (name the product, account, order or reservation it is about) and at most 30 words. Copy numbers and identifiers exactly.
- Skip greetings, questions to the customer, requests for information or confirmation, offers and instructions to the customer.
- At most 6 claims; prefer the most specific and consequential ones.
- If the message makes no factual claims, output exactly: NONE
